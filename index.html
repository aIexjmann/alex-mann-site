ALEX MANN is a writer, director, and producer.

He’s currently writing feature films for Ridley Scott’s Scott Free, Shawn Levy’s 21 Laps, and Sylvester Stallone’s Balboa Productions.

He is the founder and CEO of Space Oddity, a next-gen content studio backed by Snap Inc.

Mann’s short films have accumulated over a billion views and been featured by CNN, The Los Angeles Times and New York Magazine.

He's a sought-after commercial director for brands including Netflix, TikTok, Amazon, and Verizon.

Outside of filmmaking, Mann is a contributing writer for VICE and The New Yorker.

Mann is a member of the Writers Guild of America and represented by The Gotham Group. He lives with his wife and two children in Los Angeles.
